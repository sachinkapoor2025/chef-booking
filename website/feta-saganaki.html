Feta Saganaki

A classic Greek appetizer - golden pan-seared feta cheese with honey and sesame seeds

The Taste of Greece

Feta Saganaki is a beloved Greek appetizer that combines the salty richness of feta cheese with the sweetness of honey and the nutty crunch of sesame seeds. Our authentic preparation brings the Mediterranean flavors to your table, creating an unforgettable appetizer experience for your guests.
