Feta Saganaki

Crispy pan-fried Greek cheese - a beloved Mediterranean appetizer with golden exterior and melt-in-your-mouth interior

Greek Culinary Delight

Our Feta Saganaki is prepared using traditional Greek methods - thick slices of premium feta cheese, lightly breaded and pan-fried to golden perfection. The result is a deliciously crispy exterior with a warm, creamy interior that will delight your taste buds.
