Feta Saganaki

Greece's beloved golden fried cheese - crispy outside, creamy inside, drizzled with honey and sesame

A Greek Culinary Treasure

Feta Saganaki is one of Greece's most beloved appetizers. Premium feta cheese is lightly coated and pan-fried to golden perfection, creating a delightful contrast between the crispy exterior and the creamy, tangy interior. Finished with a drizzle of honey and sesame seeds, it's a Mediterranean delicacy that will transport your guests to the Greek islands.
