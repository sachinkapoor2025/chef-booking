About Chef Sarah

Sarah Johnson brings the heart and soul of American cuisine to your table. With over 11 years of experience, Sarah grew up in Texas learning traditional Southern cooking from her grandmother, then expanded her skills to include modern American techniques. She specializes in comfort food with a contemporary twist, from slow-smoked BBQ to gourmet burgers and everything in between.

Sarah believes that American food is about bringing people together. Whether it's a backyard BBQ or an elegant dinner party, she creates dishes that feel familiar yet exciting. Her passion for local, seasonal ingredients ensures every meal is fresh and flavorful.
