About Chef Emily

Emily Chen brings the rich and diverse flavors of Chinese cuisine to your table. Born in Shanghai and trained in both traditional and modern Chinese cooking techniques, Emily has over 12 years of experience creating authentic Chinese dishes. She specializes in dim sum, wok cooking, and regional Chinese specialties that showcase the complexity and balance of Chinese flavors.

Emily believes that Chinese cuisine is about harmony, balance, and the perfect combination of textures and flavors. She uses traditional techniques like stir-frying, steaming, and braising to create dishes that are both authentic and beautifully presented. Her passion for Chinese tea culture also influences her cooking and presentation.
