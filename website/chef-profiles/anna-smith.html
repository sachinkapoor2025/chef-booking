About Chef Anna

Anna Smith brings the elegance and sophistication of French cuisine to your table. With over 14 years of experience, Anna trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris and has worked in Michelin-starred restaurants across France and California. Her passion for French cooking is evident in every dish she creates, from delicate pastries to rich, complex sauces.

Anna specializes in classic French techniques combined with modern presentation. She believes that French cuisine is about precision, balance, and the perfect harmony of flavors. Each dish tells a story of tradition and artistry.
