About Chef Carlos

Carlos Mendez brings the vibrant and bold flavors of Mexico to your table. Born and raised in Oaxaca, Carlos learned traditional Mexican cooking from his mother and grandmother. With over 13 years of experience, he specializes in authentic Mexican dishes that showcase the rich diversity of regional Mexican cuisine, from complex moles to fresh ceviches.

Carlos believes that Mexican food is about celebration, family, and tradition. He uses traditional techniques and authentic ingredients to create dishes that are both comforting and exciting. From handmade tortillas to complex spice blends, every dish tells a story of Mexican heritage.
