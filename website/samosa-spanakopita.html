Best of Both Worlds

Experience the delightful combination of two iconic appetizers from different cultures. Our Samosas bring the authentic taste of Indian spices, while our Spanakopita offers the Mediterranean freshness of Greek cuisine. Perfect for diverse gatherings and multicultural events.

Our Offerings

Samosa

Traditional Indian pastry with crispy golden crust filled with spiced potatoes, peas, and aromatic herbs. Served with mint chutney and tamarind sauce.

Spanakopita

Classic Greek spinach pie with flaky phyllo dough filled with spinach, feta cheese, onions, and herbs. A Mediterranean delicacy that melts in your mouth.
