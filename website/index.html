Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our private chef services

How much does a private chef cost in San Francisco?

Private chef services in San Francisco typically range from $150-500+ per event depending on menu complexity, number of guests, and chef experience. Weekly meal prep starts at $200/week.

What areas do you serve in California?

We serve San Francisco, Los Angeles, San Diego, San Jose, and throughout the Bay Area. Contact us for availability in other California cities.

Can I hire a chef for weekly meal prep?

Yes! We offer weekly meal prep services where our chefs prepare healthy, customized meals for your family. Perfect for busy professionals and health-conscious individuals.

Do you provide catering for events?

Yes, we provide catering for weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, anniversaries, and special occasions throughout California.

Are your chefs background verified?

Absolutely. All our chefs undergo thorough background checks, are food safety certified, and carry proper licensing and insurance.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes! Our chefs specialize in vegan, gluten-free, keto, paleo, and other specialized diets. Just let us know your requirements when booking.
