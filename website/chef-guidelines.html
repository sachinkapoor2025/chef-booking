Food Safety & Hygiene Standards

Personal Hygiene & Handwashing

  • •
  • Wash hands thoroughly with warm water and antibacterial soap for at least 20 seconds before handling food
  • •
  • Wash hands immediately after handling raw meat, poultry, seafood, or eggs
  • •
  • Use disposable gloves when handling ready-to-eat foods and change them frequently
  • •
  • Keep fingernails short, clean, and free of nail polish
  • •
  • Wear clean chef uniforms daily and change if soiled during service
  • •
  • Use hair restraints (chef hat or hairnet) to prevent hair from contaminating food
  • •
  • Do not work with food if you have symptoms of illness (fever, vomiting, diarrhea)

Surface & Equipment Sanitation

  • •
  • Sanitize all work surfaces with food-safe sanitizer before and after food preparation
  • •
  • Use NSF-approved cutting boards and replace when heavily worn or damaged
  • •
  • Maintain separate cutting boards: RED for raw meat, YELLOW for poultry, BLUE for seafood, GREEN for vegetables
  • •
  • Sanitize knives and utensils between different food types
  • •
  • Use food-grade sanitizing solution (1 tablespoon bleach per gallon of water)
  • •
  • Allow surfaces to air dry completely after sanitizing
  • •
  • Store clean equipment on sanitized racks, not on countertops

Temperature Control & Food Storage

  • •
  • Keep cold foods at or below 40°F (4°C) using ice packs or coolers
  • •
  • Keep hot foods at or above 140°F (60°C) using chafing dishes or warming equipment
  • •
  • Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C), ground meats to 160°F (71°C), fish to 145°F (63°C)
  • •
  • Use calibrated food thermometers for accurate temperature readings
  • •
  • Follow the 2-hour rule: perishable foods should not sit at room temperature for more than 2 hours
  • •
  • Store raw meats on the bottom shelf of refrigerators to prevent cross-contamination
  • •
  • Use airtight, food-grade containers for all food storage
  • •
  • Label all containers with contents and date prepared

Cross-Contamination Prevention

  • •
  • Never place cooked foods on surfaces that previously held raw foods
  • •
  • Use separate utensils, cutting boards, and containers for raw and cooked foods
  • •
  • Store raw meats, poultry, and seafood separately from ready-to-eat foods
  • •
  • Clean and sanitize all surfaces that come into contact with raw foods
  • •
  • Wash hands thoroughly after handling raw foods before touching other ingredients
  • •
  • Use color-coded cutting boards and utensils to prevent mix-ups

Allergen Management & Special Diets

  • •
  • Obtain detailed information about client allergies and dietary restrictions in advance
  • •
  • Use dedicated equipment and preparation areas for allergen-free dishes
  • •
  • Wash hands and change gloves before preparing allergen-free meals
  • •
  • Clean and sanitize all surfaces before preparing allergen-free dishes
  • •
  • Clearly label all dishes with potential allergens (nuts, dairy, gluten, shellfish, etc.)
  • •
  • Be prepared to provide complete ingredient lists upon client request
  • •
  • Understand the severity of different allergies and take appropriate precautions
  • •
  • Never assume a client can tolerate trace amounts of allergens

Transportation & Service Safety

  • •
  • Use insulated containers to maintain proper food temperatures during transport
  • •
  • Keep hot and cold foods in separate insulated containers
  • •
  • Use clean, food-grade containers with secure lids
  • •
  • Transport raw meats in sealed containers on ice
  • •
  • Arrive at client location with food still at proper serving temperatures
  • •
  • Set up a clean, sanitized workspace in the client's kitchen
  • •
  • Use disposable gloves when plating and serving food
  • •
  • Dispose of food waste properly and clean all surfaces after service
