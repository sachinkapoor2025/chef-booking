Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to all your questions about our chef services, meal plans, and booking process

👨‍🍳 Chef Booking

How do I book a personal chef?

Booking a personal chef is simple! You can book online through our website by visiting the "Book a Chef" page, or call us directly at 408-690-1610. Our team will help you find the perfect chef based on your preferences, dietary needs, and event requirements. We'll confirm availability and provide a detailed quote before finalizing your booking.

How far in advance should I book a chef?

We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance for the best availability, especially for weekends and holidays. However, we often have chefs available on short notice for urgent requests. For large events or special occasions, we suggest booking 3-4 weeks ahead to ensure we can accommodate your specific needs and preferences.

Are your chefs verified and background checked?

Yes, absolutely! All our chefs undergo a comprehensive verification process including background checks, professional experience verification, and food safety certifications. Each chef is carefully vetted to ensure they meet our high standards for professionalism, culinary expertise, and customer service. Your safety and satisfaction are our top priorities.

Can I customize the menu with the chef?

Yes! Menu customization is one of our specialties. After booking, you'll have a consultation with your chef to discuss your preferences, dietary restrictions, and any special requirements. Whether you want to adapt traditional recipes, accommodate allergies, or create a completely unique menu, our chefs work with you to create a personalized dining experience that meets your exact needs.

What happens after I complete a chef booking?

After booking, you'll receive a confirmation email with all the details. Your assigned chef will contact you within 24-48 hours to schedule a consultation call. During this call, you'll discuss menu preferences, dietary needs, and any special requirements. The chef will provide a detailed plan and answer any questions you may have before your service date.

🍱 Weekly Meal Services

What is the weekly meal service?

Our weekly meal service provides fresh, healthy meals prepared in your kitchen by a professional chef. The chef visits your home once a week for 2-3 hours to prepare 10-12 meals that are ready to eat or easily reheated. This service includes grocery shopping, meal preparation, and cleanup, making healthy eating convenient and stress-free.

How many meals are included in weekly meal plans?

Our standard weekly meal plan includes 10-12 fresh meals per week. The exact number depends on your household size and preferences. We can customize the plan to include more or fewer meals based on your needs. Each meal is portion-controlled and designed to provide balanced nutrition throughout the week.

Can I change or pause my weekly meal service?

Yes, our weekly meal service is completely flexible. You can modify your meal plan, change the frequency, or pause the service at any time with 48 hours notice. Whether you're going on vacation, have a busy week, or want to adjust your meal preferences, we accommodate your schedule and needs without any penalties or fees.

Do chefs provide groceries for weekly meals?

Yes, grocery shopping is included in our weekly meal service price. Your chef will shop for fresh, high-quality ingredients based on your customized meal plan. We use local markets and stores to source the best seasonal ingredients. If you have specific preferences for organic, local, or specialty stores, we can accommodate those requests.

Is weekly meal service available for families?

Absolutely! Our weekly meal service is perfect for families. We can customize meals to accommodate different dietary needs and preferences within the same household. Whether you have picky eaters, dietary restrictions, or specific nutritional goals, we create balanced meals that the whole family will enjoy. We also offer kid-friendly options and can adjust portion sizes for different age groups.

🎉 Catering Services

What types of catering services do you offer?

We offer comprehensive catering services for various events including weddings, corporate functions, private parties, anniversaries, birthdays, and holiday gatherings. Our services range from elegant plated dinners to buffet-style service, cocktail parties, and family-style meals. We can accommodate events from 2 to 50 guests with fully customized menus and professional service.

Is there a minimum number of guests for catering?

Our minimum for catering services is 2 guests, making us perfect for intimate dinners and small gatherings. However, our pricing is optimized for groups of 6 or more guests. For smaller parties, we offer our one-time dining service which provides the same quality and experience at a更适合 smaller group size.

Can catering menus be customized?

Yes, all our catering menus are fully customizable. We work with you to create a menu that reflects your taste preferences, dietary needs, and event theme. Whether you want a traditional multi-course meal, fusion cuisine, or specific cultural dishes, our chefs will design a personalized menu. We also accommodate all dietary restrictions and preferences including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and more.

Do you provide serving staff and equipment for events?

Yes, our catering service includes professional serving staff and all necessary equipment. We provide tableware, glassware, linens, serving dishes, and cooking equipment. Our staff handles food service, plating, and cleanup. For larger events, we can also provide bartenders, servers, and event coordinators to ensure your event runs smoothly from start to finish.

How early should catering services be booked?

For catering services, we recommend booking 3-4 weeks in advance for the best availability and menu planning. For large events, holidays, or popular dates, booking 6-8 weeks ahead is ideal. However, we can often accommodate last-minute requests with 1-2 weeks notice, depending on chef availability and event complexity.

🥗 Diet Plan

What is the diet plan service?

Our diet plan service provides personalized nutrition guidance and meal planning tailored to your health goals, dietary needs, and lifestyle. After an initial consultation, our nutrition experts create a customized plan that considers your medical history, food preferences, allergies, and nutritional requirements. The plan includes meal suggestions, portion guidance, and ongoing support.

Do you offer diet plans for weight loss or medical needs?

Yes, we specialize in diet plans for various health goals including weight loss, diabetes management, heart health, digestive issues, food allergies, and other medical conditions. Our plans are created by certified nutrition professionals who work with your healthcare providers when necessary. We focus on sustainable, healthy eating patterns that support your overall well-being.

Are diet plans customized for individual preferences?

Absolutely! Every diet plan is completely customized to your individual needs, preferences, and lifestyle. We consider your cultural background, food likes and dislikes, cooking skills, budget, schedule, and health goals. Our plans are designed to be realistic and sustainable, not restrictive or one-size-fits-all. We believe in creating eating patterns that you can maintain long-term.

Can a chef cook meals according to my diet plan?

Yes! This is one of our most popular services. Our chefs are trained to prepare delicious meals that adhere to your specific diet plan requirements. Whether you need low-sodium, diabetic-friendly, gluten-free, or any other specialized cooking, our chefs can create flavorful meals that support your health goals. We can incorporate this into our weekly meal service or special meal preparation sessions.

How often can I update or change my diet plan?

You can update your diet plan as often as needed. We recommend regular check-ins every 4-6 weeks to assess progress and make adjustments based on your results, changing goals, or lifestyle modifications. Our nutrition experts are available for ongoing support and can modify your plan whenever you experience changes in your health status, activity level, or personal preferences.
