Payment & Policies

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. Payment is typically required at the time of booking, with flexible payment plans available for larger events. For cattering clients, we can arrange invoice billing.

Tipping is completely optional and based on your satisfaction with the service. If you're happy with your chef's performance, a tip of 15-20% is customary but never expected. The chef's service fee is included in your booking price.

We offer flexible cancellation: Full refund for cancellations 48+ hours before service, 50% refund for 24-48 hours, and no refund for cancellations within 24 hours. Rescheduling is subject to chef availability with no additional fees for changes made 48+ hours in advance.
