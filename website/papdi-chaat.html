The Crunchy Delight

Papdi Chaat is a popular Indian street food featuring crispy, deep-fried wafers (papdis) topped with whipped yogurt, tangy tamarind chutney, spicy green chutney, and a variety of fresh ingredients. It's a delightful combination of textures and flavors that creates a memorable snacking experience for any occasion.
