How It's Prepared

Fresh Vegetables

Cucumber is lattice-cut for that perfect crunch, while tomatoes and bell peppers are fresh sliced for maximum flavor.

Quality Feta

Premium Greek feta cheese is cubed and added to the salad for that creamy, tangy taste.

Mixed Olives

A blend of kalamata and green olives are added for authentic Mediterranean flavor.

Perfect Seasoning

Fresh oregano and sea salt are added, then drizzled with olive oil and lemon juice.
