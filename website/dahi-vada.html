The King of Indian Chaat

Dahi Vada, also known as Dahi Bhalla, is a beloved Indian delicacy featuring soft lentil fritters soaked in whipped yogurt, garnished with tangy tamarind chutney, spicy green chutney, and aromatic spices. It's the perfect blend of textures and flavors - soft, creamy, tangy, and spicy all at once.

Our Preparation Process

1

Soaking & Grinding

Urad dal is soaked overnight and ground to a fluffy batter with perfect aeration.

2

Frying to Perfection

Batter is shaped and deep-fried until golden brown and crispy on the outside.

3

Soaking Process

Fried vadas are soaked in water to soften and then transferred to spiced yogurt.

4

Final Garnish

Topped with chutneys, spices, sev, and fresh coriander for the perfect finish.
